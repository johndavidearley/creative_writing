# Black Banner Academy: Chapter Outlines By Shape

## Option 1: The Covert Recruitment Pipeline

1. **The Flagship Problem**: Lio sacrifices the flagship in simulation and attracts Black Banner attention.
2. **The Interview Without Questions**: The first assessment tests secrecy and compromised loyalty.
3. **A Better Kind Of Alone**: Black Banner gives Lio belonging by separating him from Iven and ordinary cadets.
4. **Live Codes**: The cohort discovers its exercises use real fleet logistics and restricted systems.
5. **Useful Violations**: Senn proves the program is using illegal surveillance to build cadet profiles.
6. **The Patron Dinner**: Alumni and covert handlers flatter the cadets with adult fear and secret responsibility.
7. **Iven's Evidence**: Iven finds proof that Black Banner assessments are not academy-authorized.
8. **Continuity Scenario**: A simulated constitutional crisis includes real yard access and detention protocols.
9. **The Crisis Goes Live**: Black Banner is ordered to secure orbital yards during an engineered emergency.
10. **Who Authorized This?**: Lio realizes the battle is over authority, not control of the yards.
11. **Public Systems**: He turns academy training networks into a live evidentiary broadcast.
12. **Career Death**: The pipeline is exposed; Lio saves the academy's legitimacy and loses his command future.

## Option 2: The Cadet Coup

1. **The Flagship Problem**: Lio and Quell are selected after opposing simulation answers.
2. **Black Banner**: The cohort is told it exists for moments when civilian authority fails.
3. **Secret Access**: Cadets receive emergency permissions that feel like trust.
4. **Rook's Moment**: Rook excels at decisive action and becomes the cohort's emotional center.
5. **Senn's Doubt**: Senn notices authority layers that should not exist in academy systems.
6. **Iven Outside The Door**: Iven, excluded, finds constitutional records contradicting the scenario.
7. **Manufactured Treason**: Lio learns the ministerial threat may be exaggerated.
8. **Lockdown Order**: Black Banner is ordered to secure the academy and detain civilian inspectors.
9. **Cohort Split**: Rook obeys; Quell hesitates; Senn sabotages access; Lio chooses the oath.
10. **Yard Fight**: Cadets battle cadets through drones, locks, and training vessels.
11. **The Failed Coup**: The illegal seizure is stopped from inside.
12. **Aftermath Of Friends**: The cohort fractures into heroes, criminals, and future enemies.

## Option 3: The School That Makes Monsters

1. **The Flagship Problem**: Lio wins by ugly brilliance and wants recognition.
2. **Highest Proof**: Black Banner appears to validate him as more than a quota cadet.
3. **Secret As Virtue**: The first test rewards hiding truth from a friend.
4. **Outcome Over Law**: The second test rewards mission success through illegal means.
5. **Loyalty Over Truth**: The cohort protects one of its own from consequences.
6. **Confidence Over Doubt**: Rook is rewarded for acting before he understands.
7. **Wren's Warning**: Wren tells Lio the program does not test character; it reshapes it.
8. **The Cadets Change**: Black Banner members become effective, admired, and less trustworthy.
9. **Final Test Setup**: A public crisis demands a brilliant off-book solution.
10. **Refusing Brilliance**: Lio chooses procedural accountability when everyone expects genius.
11. **Public Failure**: His apparent failure exposes the hidden curriculum and prevents deaths.
12. **The Right Kind Of Shame**: Lio accepts disgrace rather than become the academy's monster.

## Option 4: The Rival Protagonists

1. **Two Answers**: Lio sacrifices the flagship; Quell preserves it and loses the convoy.
2. **Two Invitations**: Both enter Black Banner for opposite reasons.
3. **Pedigree And Deniability**: Anik frames Lio as useful in shadows and Quell as useful in public.
4. **Competition Track**: Tests pit improvisation against legitimacy.
5. **Envy**: Lio envies Quell's credibility; Quell envies Lio's freedom from family mythology.
6. **The Trap For Lio**: He is pushed toward deniable action.
7. **The Trap For Quell**: She is asked to legitimize something she privately doubts.
8. **Mutual Recognition**: Each sees how Black Banner is exploiting the other's wound.
9. **Evidence And Name**: Lio gets proof; Quell realizes only her family name can force review.
10. **Burning The Name**: Quell publicly validates Lio's evidence against her own patrons.
11. **No Revenge**: Lio protects cadets still inside instead of destroying the academy wholesale.
12. **A Different Cohort**: Their partnership becomes the model for lawful dissent.

## Option 5: The Handler's Success

1. **The Flagship Problem**: Lio's ugly tactical clarity interests Anik.
2. **The Test That Rewards Resistance**: Lio refuses the first dirty premise, which Anik likes.
3. **Suspicion As Trait**: Every time Lio investigates, the program quietly advances him.
4. **Wren's Warning**: Wren tells him opposition can become recruitment.
5. **The Visible Faction**: Lio finds a reckless Black Banner cell preparing illegal action.
6. **Useful Discovery**: Anik lets him gather real evidence because the cell needs pruning.
7. **The Scandal Plan**: Lio thinks exposure will destroy Black Banner.
8. **Allowed Victory**: His leak detonates exactly where Anik needs it to.
9. **Clean Institution**: The academy sacrifices visible offenders and claims reform.
10. **The Deeper Service**: Anik reveals the real network survived and is now cleaner.
11. **The Real Invitation**: Lio is invited to hunt hidden abuses from within.
12. **Unresolved Door**: Lio refuses, accepts, or postpones the answer, knowing all choices are contaminated.

## Option 6: The Academy Under Siege

1. **The Flagship Problem**: Academy rivalry and tactical culture are established.
2. **Black Banner Invitation**: Lio enters the elite cohort.
3. **Illegal Preparation**: The cadets learn methods officially forbidden to students.
4. **Civilian Inspectors**: Oversight arrives, threatening to expose the program.
5. **First Blast**: Sabotage or attack traps the academy in orbit.
6. **Official Systems Fail**: Communications, command chains, and emergency procedures collapse.
7. **Black Banner Works**: The cohort's illegal training saves lives.
8. **Hero Narrative**: Cadets become public heroes while Wren sees the danger.
9. **Expansion Proposal**: Orison argues the crisis proves Black Banner should grow.
10. **Truth Versus Victory**: Lio finds proof the crisis was enabled or worsened by the program's secrecy.
11. **Tarnish The Rescue**: He must expose the truth behind a victory people need to believe in.
12. **Necessary Is Not Good**: The academy survives, but Lio refuses to let saved lives sanctify corruption.
