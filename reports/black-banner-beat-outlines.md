# Black Banner Academy: Beat Outlines

Each chapter is subdivided into `Pressure`, `Turn`, and `Exit` beats.

## Option 1: The Covert Recruitment Pipeline

1. **The Flagship Problem**
   - Pressure: Lio must solve an impossible fleet simulation.
   - Turn: He sacrifices the flagship to save civilians.
   - Exit: Black Banner notices him.
2. **The Interview Without Questions**
   - Pressure: Lio is tested with a friend's real violation.
   - Turn: He refuses the dirty process, not the moral problem.
   - Exit: Anik identifies resistance as useful.
3. **A Better Kind Of Alone**
   - Pressure: Black Banner offers Lio belonging.
   - Turn: Acceptance isolates him from Iven.
   - Exit: Secrecy starts costing friendship.
4. **Live Codes**
   - Pressure: Training scenarios feel too accurate.
   - Turn: Senn proves real fleet codes are involved.
   - Exit: The cohort has crossed from school into operations.
5. **Useful Violations**
   - Pressure: Cadets learn private flaws are being cataloged.
   - Turn: Senn finds illegal surveillance.
   - Exit: The program can control anyone it selects.
6. **The Patron Dinner**
   - Pressure: Alumni flatter the cadets with adult fear.
   - Turn: Lio realizes secrecy is being sold as maturity.
   - Exit: He wants the approval anyway.
7. **Iven's Evidence**
   - Pressure: Iven investigates from outside.
   - Turn: He finds proof Black Banner lacks authorization.
   - Exit: Lio must choose whether to protect him.
8. **Continuity Scenario**
   - Pressure: A constitutional crisis scenario begins.
   - Turn: The scenario includes real detention protocols.
   - Exit: The exercise can become action instantly.
9. **The Crisis Goes Live**
   - Pressure: Emergency orders arrive.
   - Turn: Black Banner is told to secure the yards.
   - Exit: Lio sees the pipeline activating.
10. **Who Authorized This?**
   - Pressure: Cadets demand quick action.
   - Turn: Lio asks who has lawful authority.
   - Exit: The cohort splits around that question.
11. **Public Systems**
   - Pressure: Hidden orders must stay hidden to work.
   - Turn: Lio broadcasts evidence through academy systems.
   - Exit: Secrecy becomes impossible.
12. **Career Death**
   - Pressure: The institution survives by punishing exposure.
   - Turn: Lio accepts being marked disloyal.
   - Exit: He loses the career he wanted but keeps the oath.
