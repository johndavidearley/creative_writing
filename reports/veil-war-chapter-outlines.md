# The Veil War: Chapter Outlines By Shape

## Option 1: The Refugee Fleet War

1. **Neutral Bell**: Halcyon takes in the Mirrorglass convoy and refuses to surrender Nima Vale.
2. **Old Enemies At The Dock**: The Kharu carrier arrives; Mara grants repair-right and turns asylum into a faction crisis.
3. **Eight Thousand Mouths**: Food, air, quarantine, and sleeping space become political weapons inside Halcyon.
4. **The First Offer**: Veyr offers medicine and safe conduct to any ship willing to detach and surrender Nima.
5. **Edited Loyalties**: Elian finds treaty marks in refugees who sincerely argue for Concordance protection.
6. **The Riot Of Blue Line**: Refugees and Kharu clash; Mara imposes emergency movement controls.
7. **Emergency Rule**: Mara saves the fleet from sabotage but loses civilian trust and gives Veyr propaganda.
8. **The Witness Refuses**: Nima reveals enough of the rite-logic to prove the Concordance rewrites kinship.
9. **No One Believes Command**: Halcyon's factions reject Mara's next order because emergency rule has poisoned consent.
10. **The False Allegiance**: Mara uses corrupted Concordance rite-logic to falsify Halcyon's absorbed status.
11. **Lowered Shields**: The enemy fleet accepts the false status long enough for Halcyon to break formation and flee.
12. **Survival's Stain**: The fleet survives, but Mara must answer for using identity violation as a weapon.

## Option 2: The Carrier Alliance Campaign

1. **Neutral Bell**: Refugees arrive under pursuit; Mara grants asylum.
2. **Blood-Of-Ash**: Rauk var Kesh limps in with a crippled Kharu carrier and demands repair-right.
3. **Blue Line**: Mara creates strict zones to prevent refugee/Kharu violence.
4. **Mixed Squadron**: Human, Kharu, and free-crew pilots are forced into a joint patrol.
5. **First Sortie**: The patrol barely survives; Jace Myles becomes famous for an impulsive save.
6. **Propaganda Ace**: Jace's fame is used by Halcyon and exploited by Concordance counter-messaging.
7. **Honor Versus Patience**: Rauk demands retaliation after a Concordance probe kills Kharu wounded.
8. **Sabotaged Deck**: Concordance agents provoke an internal attack on the Kharu launch bay.
9. **The Spine Target**: Mara learns the enemy's real objective is Halcyon's repair spine, not Nima alone.
10. **Carrier Sacrifice**: Mara and Rauk turn the damaged Blood-of-Ash deck into a trap.
11. **The Last Launch**: The mixed squadron saves the civilian fleet while the Kharu carrier is gutted.
12. **A Real Alliance**: Rauk accepts the cost; Jace dies, vanishes, or is captured, giving the next book its wound.

## Option 3: The Occupation Without Invasion

1. **Asylum Claim**: Halcyon accepts Nima and the refugee convoy.
2. **The Legal Observer**: Veyr stays nearby, claiming peaceful oversight.
3. **Mercy Contracts**: Concordance aid offers begin arriving for individual ships.
4. **Voting With Airlocks**: The first free-crew vessel detaches voluntarily.
5. **Relief Or Surrender**: Refugees argue that Concordance order is better than Mara's dangerous principles.
6. **The Soft Occupation**: Edited-loyalty survivors sincerely testify that they were saved, not conquered.
7. **Mara's Bad Choice**: Mara considers coercing ships to stay and realizes that would prove Veyr right.
8. **The Oath-Ledger**: Ilyan and Elian identify a public rite that can test consent without military force.
9. **The Tribunal**: Kharu, refugees, smugglers, and officers testify under Lumen oath.
10. **Consent Exposed**: The Concordance's peace is shown to depend on altered personhood.
11. **The Fleet Vote**: Halcyon chooses collective flight, not because Mara commands it but because witnesses persuade them.
12. **Government In Exile**: Halcyon leaves as a moving political body with fragile legitimacy.

## Option 4: The Witness-Child Betrayal

1. **The Forbidden Child**: Mara refuses to surrender Nima.
2. **The Chapel Enclave**: Ilyan hides Nima while Elian begins oath-ledger work.
3. **Useful Testimony**: Nima reveals a Concordance route or code that saves lives.
4. **Contradictions**: Her account changes in small, impossible ways.
5. **Multiple Selves**: Elian discovers Nima carries overwritten legal identities.
6. **Trust Fractures**: Halcyon's factions argue Nima is a weapon and should be contained.
7. **The Concordance Waits**: Veyr does not attack, suggesting retrieval was never the only goal.
8. **Buried Phrase**: A phrase in a routine broadcast nearly makes Nima open a restricted system.
9. **Containment Order**: Mara is pressured to sedate or isolate Nima before battle.
10. **Witnessed Oath**: Mara lets Nima make a Lumen oath that establishes her chosen legal self.
11. **Defense By Personhood**: Nima resists the trigger and helps seal Halcyon's grid.
12. **Not A Symbol**: Mara protects Nima from becoming propaganda for either side.

## Option 5: The Fall Of Halcyon

1. **Fleet-City**: Halcyon is introduced as the only repair refuge on the collapsing front.
2. **Too Many Saved**: Refugees, Kharu, and free crews overwhelm the city's systems.
3. **The First Detachment**: A ship leaves for Concordance protection.
4. **Repair Spine Sabotage**: Halcyon's shared infrastructure becomes the obvious target.
5. **Emergency Rule**: Mara centralizes authority and accelerates internal fracture.
6. **The Empty Core Theory**: Quill argues Halcyon is not the metal but the network of crews.
7. **Target Concentration**: The Concordance commits to taking the fleet-city whole.
8. **Impossible Defense**: Mara realizes defending the core will kill the people it exists to protect.
9. **Break The City**: Mara secretly prepares the dispersal of Halcyon's modules and dependent ships.
10. **Declared Defeat**: She abandons the core and lets the Concordance capture it.
11. **Many Halcyons**: The dispersed convoys carry testimony, repair crews, and law in fragments.
12. **Victory Misnamed**: The Concordance claims triumph over an empty hull while Halcyon survives as a distributed polity.
