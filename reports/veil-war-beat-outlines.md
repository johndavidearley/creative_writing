# The Veil War: Beat Outlines

Each chapter is subdivided into `Pressure`, `Turn`, and `Exit` beats.

## Option 1: The Refugee Fleet War

1. **Neutral Bell**
   - Pressure: The Mirrorglass convoy arrives damaged, overcrowded, and pursued.
   - Turn: Mara grants asylum to all aboard, including Nima.
   - Exit: Veyr identifies Nima as the condition for peace.
2. **Old Enemies At The Dock**
   - Pressure: The Kharu carrier demands repair-right.
   - Turn: Mara admits them despite refugee outrage.
   - Exit: Concordance agents use the decision to frame Mara as reckless.
3. **Eight Thousand Mouths**
   - Pressure: Food, air, quarantine, and sleep space run short.
   - Turn: Quill exposes that some ships are hoarding emergency reserves.
   - Exit: Mara must ration publicly or lose control privately.
4. **The First Offer**
   - Pressure: Veyr offers aid to ships that detach.
   - Turn: One ship seriously considers leaving.
   - Exit: Nima's protection becomes a fleet-wide referendum.
5. **Edited Loyalties**
   - Pressure: Elian finds treaty marks inside refugee nerves.
   - Turn: The marked refugees sincerely defend Concordance rule.
   - Exit: Mara realizes the enemy can create loyal victims.
6. **The Riot Of Blue Line**
   - Pressure: Refugees confront Kharu wounded near a restricted zone.
   - Turn: Violence erupts after a planted rumor.
   - Exit: Mara imposes emergency movement controls.
7. **Emergency Rule**
   - Pressure: Sabotage threatens life support and repair arms.
   - Turn: Mara centralizes authority and saves the fleet.
   - Exit: Her own people begin fearing her.
8. **The Witness Refuses**
   - Pressure: Nima is asked to explain the rites.
   - Turn: She refuses to become a tool unless granted public witness status.
   - Exit: Mara must restore agency to someone everyone wants to use.
9. **No One Believes Command**
   - Pressure: Mara orders a dangerous maneuver.
   - Turn: Civilian captains refuse because emergency rule broke trust.
   - Exit: Mara needs a plan that does not depend on belief.
10. **The False Allegiance**
   - Pressure: The Concordance fleet closes.
   - Turn: Mara uses rite-logic to fake Halcyon's absorbed status.
   - Exit: The enemy accepts the lie, briefly.
11. **Lowered Shields**
   - Pressure: Halcyon has minutes before the deception fails.
   - Turn: The fleet breaks formation through the opening.
   - Exit: Mara escapes but leaves evidence of her violation.
12. **Survival's Stain**
   - Pressure: Survivors demand accountability.
   - Turn: Mara admits what she did without defending it as clean.
   - Exit: The fleet survives under a commander no one can fully absolve.

## Option 2: The Carrier Alliance Campaign

1. **Neutral Bell**
   - Pressure: Refugees arrive and overload Halcyon's repair capacity.
   - Turn: Mara grants asylum.
   - Exit: Concordance pursuit signatures remain nearby.
2. **Blood-Of-Ash**
   - Pressure: Rauk's damaged carrier requests repair-right.
   - Turn: Mara accepts the Kharu into the perimeter.
   - Exit: Refugees threaten riot.
3. **Blue Line**
   - Pressure: Human and Kharu wounded are placed dangerously close.
   - Turn: Mara creates strict movement zones.
   - Exit: Both sides see the line as insult.
4. **Mixed Squadron**
   - Pressure: Halcyon lacks enough pilots for patrol.
   - Turn: Mara orders a mixed human/Kharu/free-crew squadron.
   - Exit: Jace and a Kharu ace nearly kill each other in training.
5. **First Sortie**
   - Pressure: Concordance probes test the perimeter.
   - Turn: Jace makes an impulsive save that works.
   - Exit: He becomes famous for the wrong lesson.
6. **Propaganda Ace**
   - Pressure: Halcyon needs morale.
   - Turn: Command uses Jace's sortie footage.
   - Exit: Veyr reframes him as proof Halcyon spends children.
7. **Honor Versus Patience**
   - Pressure: Concordance kills Kharu wounded in a precision strike.
   - Turn: Rauk demands retaliation now.
   - Exit: Mara delays him and risks losing the alliance.
8. **Sabotaged Deck**
   - Pressure: The Kharu launch bay is attacked from inside.
   - Turn: Evidence points to a refugee, then to Concordance manipulation.
   - Exit: Mara has to defend the accused from both sides.
9. **The Spine Target**
   - Pressure: Repair systems fail in a pattern.
   - Turn: Sera proves the enemy targets Halcyon's repair spine.
   - Exit: Mara knows the next attack will force carrier sacrifice.
10. **Carrier Sacrifice**
   - Pressure: The Concordance strike begins.
   - Turn: Rauk agrees to turn Blood-of-Ash into the trap.
   - Exit: Jace volunteers for the last launch.
11. **The Last Launch**
   - Pressure: Mixed squadrons must hold until the trap fires.
   - Turn: The damaged deck detonates at the right moment.
   - Exit: Jace is lost, captured, or presumed dead.
12. **A Real Alliance**
   - Pressure: Victory grief threatens to sour into blame.
   - Turn: Rauk publicly names the sacrifice honorable and shared.
   - Exit: The alliance becomes real because it has buried dead together.

## Option 3: The Occupation Without Invasion

1. **Asylum Claim**
   - Pressure: Halcyon accepts refugees and Nima.
   - Turn: Veyr does not attack.
   - Exit: The enemy chooses law instead of weapons.
2. **The Legal Observer**
   - Pressure: Veyr requests observer status.
   - Turn: Mara cannot reject it without looking unlawful.
   - Exit: Concordance presence becomes normalized.
3. **Mercy Contracts**
   - Pressure: Individual ships receive relief offers.
   - Turn: One hungry captain signs.
   - Exit: The fleet learns leaving is possible.
4. **Voting With Airlocks**
   - Pressure: More ships debate detachment.
   - Turn: A free-crew vessel leaves peacefully.
   - Exit: Mara's authority visibly shrinks.
5. **Relief Or Surrender**
   - Pressure: Refugees accuse Mara of choosing principle over survival.
   - Turn: Edited survivors testify that Concordance rule saved them.
   - Exit: The moral argument begins moving against Mara.
6. **The Soft Occupation**
   - Pressure: Concordance aid stations outperform Halcyon's systems.
   - Turn: Ilyan discovers gratitude has been ritually altered.
   - Exit: Consent itself becomes suspect.
7. **Mara's Bad Choice**
   - Pressure: Advisors urge Mara to forbid detachment.
   - Turn: She realizes coercion proves Veyr's point.
   - Exit: She chooses persuasion over force, at a cost.
8. **The Oath-Ledger**
   - Pressure: The fleet needs a shared test for consent.
   - Turn: Elian proposes a public Lumen testimony rite.
   - Exit: Veyr cannot oppose it without revealing fear.
9. **The Tribunal**
   - Pressure: Witnesses from hostile factions must speak together.
   - Turn: A Kharu enemy corroborates refugee testimony.
   - Exit: The Concordance peace is exposed as manufactured consent.
10. **Consent Exposed**
   - Pressure: Veyr tries to discredit the rite.
   - Turn: A marked survivor reverses their testimony.
   - Exit: The fleet sees occupation's inner mechanism.
11. **The Fleet Vote**
   - Pressure: Halcyon must choose before supplies fail.
   - Turn: Captains vote for collective flight.
   - Exit: Mara commands by consent for the first time.
12. **Government In Exile**
   - Pressure: Flight means abandoning stability.
   - Turn: Halcyon formalizes itself as a moving polity.
   - Exit: The war becomes political survival, not defense of a place.

## Option 4: The Witness-Child Betrayal

1. **The Forbidden Child**
   - Pressure: Nima is demanded by the Concordance.
   - Turn: Mara refuses surrender.
   - Exit: Nima becomes Halcyon's moral center.
2. **The Chapel Enclave**
   - Pressure: Nima needs protection from allies and enemies.
   - Turn: Ilyan shelters her under Lumen law.
   - Exit: Military authority and sacred law collide.
3. **Useful Testimony**
   - Pressure: Halcyon needs actionable intelligence.
   - Turn: Nima gives a code that saves lives.
   - Exit: Trust in her increases.
4. **Contradictions**
   - Pressure: Details in Nima's account shift.
   - Turn: Elian proves the contradictions are not lies.
   - Exit: Nima may contain multiple imposed selves.
5. **Multiple Selves**
   - Pressure: Each identity state carries different legal claims.
   - Turn: One state recognizes Concordance authority.
   - Exit: Halcyon debates whether Nima can consent.
6. **Trust Fractures**
   - Pressure: Factions demand containment.
   - Turn: Nima harms no one but cannot explain herself.
   - Exit: Mara delays containment and spends political capital.
7. **The Concordance Waits**
   - Pressure: Veyr refuses to attack.
   - Turn: The delay suggests Nima is already positioned as a trigger.
   - Exit: Protection may be exactly what the enemy wants.
8. **Buried Phrase**
   - Pressure: A routine broadcast activates Nima briefly.
   - Turn: She nearly opens a restricted system.
   - Exit: Containment becomes the rational option.
9. **Containment Order**
   - Pressure: Mara's staff demands sedation.
   - Turn: Mara refuses to reduce Nima to risk.
   - Exit: She seeks a legal/personhood solution.
10. **Witnessed Oath**
   - Pressure: The battle starts before the rite is stable.
   - Turn: Nima chooses one sworn self in public.
   - Exit: The Concordance trigger loses authority.
11. **Defense By Personhood**
   - Pressure: The defense grid is under attack.
   - Turn: Nima uses her chosen oath to seal the breach.
   - Exit: Halcyon survives because Nima is treated as a person.
12. **Not A Symbol**
   - Pressure: Everyone wants to claim Nima's meaning.
   - Turn: Mara protects her from both propaganda and sainthood.
   - Exit: The war's moral center remains human-scale.

## Option 5: The Fall Of Halcyon

1. **Fleet-City**
   - Pressure: Halcyon is introduced as the only repair refuge.
   - Turn: The refugee convoy doubles its burden.
   - Exit: The city can no longer function normally.
2. **Too Many Saved**
   - Pressure: Every rescue worsens scarcity.
   - Turn: Kharu and free crews enter the same stressed systems.
   - Exit: Halcyon's strength becomes fragility.
3. **The First Detachment**
   - Pressure: A captain chooses Concordance protection.
   - Turn: Mara lets them go.
   - Exit: Others see departure as legitimate.
4. **Repair Spine Sabotage**
   - Pressure: Shared infrastructure begins failing.
   - Turn: The sabotage reveals overcentralization.
   - Exit: One strike can kill everyone.
5. **Emergency Rule**
   - Pressure: Mara centralizes control to save lives.
   - Turn: She prevents collapse.
   - Exit: The fleet starts associating unity with coercion.
6. **The Empty Core Theory**
   - Pressure: Quill challenges Mara's fixation on the core.
   - Turn: She argues Halcyon is crews, not metal.
   - Exit: Mara resists because abandonment feels like failure.
7. **Target Concentration**
   - Pressure: The Concordance commits to taking Halcyon whole.
   - Turn: Their plan depends on everyone staying gathered.
   - Exit: Mara sees the trap.
8. **Impossible Defense**
   - Pressure: Defending the core will cost the civilians.
   - Turn: Mara decides not to defend it.
   - Exit: She prepares a defeat no one can understand.
9. **Break The City**
   - Pressure: Modules and ships must separate under secrecy.
   - Turn: Quill coordinates the dispersal.
   - Exit: Halcyon begins dying as an object.
10. **Declared Defeat**
   - Pressure: Concordance forces arrive.
   - Turn: Mara lets them take the empty core.
   - Exit: The enemy broadcasts victory.
11. **Many Halcyons**
   - Pressure: Dispersed convoys struggle alone.
   - Turn: Shared testimony and repair crews keep them connected.
   - Exit: Halcyon survives as a network.
12. **Victory Misnamed**
   - Pressure: Mara is blamed for losing the city.
   - Turn: Survivors understand the loss was strategy.
   - Exit: The next war is fought by an idea, not a fortress.
